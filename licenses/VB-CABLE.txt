============================================================

The origin of VB-CABLE : www.vb-cable.com

How CamMixer distributes it
---------------------------

The Windows installer of CamMixer contains the official VB-CABLE package (VBCABLE_Driver_Pack45.zip,
downloaded from https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ and copied unchanged) and offers to install it, as an
optional task, when VB-CABLE is not already on the computer. VB-CABLE is a product of VB-Audio
Software, not of CamMixer: it is shown with its name and origin in the installer, and you can donate
or buy a licence on https://vb-audio.com/Cable/ (Donate button) if you find it useful.

https://shop.vb-audio.com.

Removing it
-----------

Windows: Settings > Apps > "VBCABLE, The Virtual Audio Cable" > Uninstall (the entry added by
VB-Audio's setup), or run VBCABLE_Setup_x64.exe as administrator and press "Remove Driver"; then
restart Windows. When you uninstall CamMixer, it asks whether to remove VB-CABLE too if it was
installed by the CamMixer installer.
